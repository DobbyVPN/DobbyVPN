<p>Open-source VPN client for Outline/Shadowsocks, Xray, and TrustTunnel.</p>

<p><strong>Supported protocols:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Outline/Shadowsocks, including WebSocket</li>
  <li>Xray</li>
  <li>TrustTunnel</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Configuration</strong></p>
<p>Consume subscription or dynamic-key configuration as TOML via HTTPS or inline. See the <a href="https://github.com/DobbyVPN/DobbyVPN/blob/main/README.md">project README</a> for the current format and examples.</p>
